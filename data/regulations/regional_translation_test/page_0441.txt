Subject :- Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR)
Regarding clarification on Regulation No. 14.2.1 of the approved regulations.
Sir,
Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations have been approved with the note that Regulation No. 14.2.1 - Transit Oriented Development for Pune Municipal Corporation area is deferred. The said regulations have been approved with the note that the said regulation is deferred. The Maharashtra Government has come to the notice that there is a question regarding the implementation of the said deferred regulation.
In view of the above-mentioned question, I have been directed to provide the clarification as follows under Regulation No. 1.9(v):
"The approved Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) Regulation No. 14.2.1 - Transit Oriented Development for Pune Municipal Corporation area is deferred. However, the Maharashtra Government has issued the amended Regulation through Notification No. TPS-1818/150/Pr. No. 158/19/Navi-13 dated 30/06/2020 under the Maharashtra Regional Planning and Urban Development Act, 1966, Section 37(1ka) for the purpose of amendment of Regulation No. 24.8 of the previously approved Development Control and Promotion Regulations - 2017. The said amended Regulation has been published and implemented immediately on 30/06/2020 as per the directions under Section 154. Therefore, the said Regulation implemented as per the said directions will remain in force. That is, the approved TOD Regulation will continue to be in force as before."
