For the areas, in accordance with the reference No. 1 of the notification for providing basic amenities, it is necessary to levy the enhanced rate as per the government's opinion. In the notification No. TPS-2416/Pr. No. 43/16/Nav-9 dated 20/4/2016, the new currency No. 'K-1' is being incorporated below the currency No. 'K' as follows:
"K-1) The Authorities shall, in accordance with the provisions of the regulation No. 2.3 (vi) of the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations, while amending the jurisdiction of the land use department, carry out the action of levying the enhanced rate as per the above 'A'."
The above amended provision is being issued to all planning authorities/urban development departments of the state to bring into force from the date of this notification, in accordance with the provisions of the Act No. 154, to issue the directive.
