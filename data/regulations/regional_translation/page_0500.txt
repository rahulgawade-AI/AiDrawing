Order
Maharashtra Regional Planning and Urban Development Act, 1966, preamble -
The Addendum to model building Bye-Laws 2016 issued by the Central Government's Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs shall be incorporated immediately in the approved development control regulations of all planning authorities in the state.
The provisions of the Addendum to model building Bye-Laws 2016 are related to protection of buildings, therefore, necessary action under New-1 is required, hence, for the public interest, the implementation of the Central Government's guiding instructions shall be ensured from this perspective, and for the purpose of applying the guiding principles and regulations of the Addendum to model building Bye-Laws 2016 throughout the state, the Government is considering issuing the directive under Section 154 of the Maharashtra Regional Planning and Urban Development Act, 1966 to all planning authorities in the state.
Following this, for the public interest, the Government is issuing the directive under Section 154 of the Maharashtra Regional Planning and Urban Development Act, 1966 to all planning authorities in the state to immediately incorporate the Addendum to model building Bye-Laws 2016 issued by the Central Government's Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs in the approved development control regulations of all planning authorities in the state.
