Therefore, from 11.01.1967 onwards but until 02.12.2020, if unauthorized plots have been formed without obtaining permission from the competent authority, and if the area of such a plot is less than 0.40 hectares, then the three options under regulation 3.4.1(i) will remain available. However, from 03.12.2020 onwards, if unauthorized plots have been formed without obtaining permission from the competent authority, and even if their area is less than 0.40 hectares, then in such proposals, only option (a) under regulation 3.4.1(i) will remain available, i.e., the total area of such a plot will be released as open space at 10% or a minimum of 200 square meters. The other two options (b) and (c) will no longer be available.
