Government Order
1. All planning authorities in the state shall implement the Addendum to model building Bye-Laws 2016 issued by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Central Government, as per the annexure, and ensure compliance with the provisions relating to the Development Control Regulations in the said annexure. 2. This directive shall come into effect immediately and all planning authorities in the state shall immediately commence the implementation of the said regulations of the Government.
In accordance with the orders of the Governor of Maharashtra, by name.
(Dr. Pratibha Bhadane)
Sahayak Secretary, Maharashtra Government
