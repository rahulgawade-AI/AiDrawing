and whereby, in accordance with the provision No. 1.9 (v) of the said regulations, there is a provision regarding the issuance of orders by the government in relation to the question/point in question under the application of various provisions of UDCPR;
and whereby, the government has issued guidance to the concerned persons in accordance with the said provision and the timely issued orders;
and whereby, a report has been received by the planning authority from the government regarding the difficulties arising in the implementation of the provisions of regulation No. 11.2.5 of the said regulations in relation to the development of the road proposed under the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act, 1949 for the purpose of Construction Amenity TDR;
and whereby, considering the said report and in the interest of public, the government has formed the opinion that it is necessary to review such provisions of the said regulations and to clarify such provisions in accordance with regulation No. 1.9 (v) of the said regulations for the effective implementation of the said regulations;
now, therefore, the following explanatory instructions are issued in accordance with regulation No. 11.2.5 of the said regulations :-
"Where the proposed road in the approved development plan or the proposed road under the Municipal Corporation Act, the landowner of the same location shall transfer the land under the road to the authority and develop it, the landowner shall be entitled to both types of TDR under regulation No. 11.2.2 and regulation No. 11.2.5. However, where the construction of the road is carried out by a third-party developer other than the landowner, the provision regarding such road construction is limited to the development plan road only."
