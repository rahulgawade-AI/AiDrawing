Sir,
By virtue of the above letter, Commissioner, Panvel Municipal Corporation has requested the Government for guidance regarding the methodology to be followed for granting construction permits under the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) for plots allocated to Panvel Municipal Corporation by the SIDCO Authority. As the situation regarding the matter is also present in the New Mumbai Municipal Corporation, I have been directed to provide the following guidance to both Municipal Corporations under Rule No. 1.9 (V) of the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations.
Serial No.
Panvel Municipal Corporation's matter
Guidance on the matter

1.
As per Clause 2.1.14 of the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations, it is specified that the common share of the total premium charge shall be 50% to the Government and 50% to the Planning Authority. As per the above specification, even though SIDCO is the landowner, since the Planning Authority is providing the basic infrastructure/services in that area, the Planning Authority, i.e., Panvel Municipal Corporation, shall be entitled to 50% of the total valuation amount, and the remaining 25% of the valuation amount shall be payable to SIDCO as the landowner, and the remaining 25% of the valuation amount shall be payable to the Government.
