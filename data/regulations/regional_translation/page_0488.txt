Prior to the application of the said regulations, temporary approval was granted to the 'D' category municipal corporations in accordance with the provisions of the applicable regulations, keeping the amenity space reserved for the purpose of site plan approval. Can such cases also be granted revised approval or how?
As per the amendment notification dated 16.06.2021 under regulation 3.5.1 of the said regulations, it is mandatory to reserve 5% facility area in the layout / subdivision of plots of 20000 sq.m. or more. The facility area reserved as per the existing regulations in the layout / subdivision proposals approved prior to 16.06.2021 (temporary or final layout) shall not be reduced while revising the layout in accordance with the said regulations, considering the provisions of regulation 3.5.1(viii).

Since the said regulations came into force from 03.12.2020, will the options (a), (b), (c) under regulation 3.4.1(i) of the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations continue to apply to cases where plots of area less than 0.4 hectare are divided into smaller plots for the purpose of site plan approval after that date or how?
After 11.01.1967, if any land has been divided into unauthorized plots without the permission of the competent authority, and the area of such plot is less than 0.40 hectare, then while preparing the layout / subdivision of such plot, three options (a), (b), (c) are mentioned under regulation 3.4.1(i), and the applicant has the right to choose any one of these options. Accordingly, the planning authority shall take into consideration the option chosen by the applicant and take a decision on the proposals received by the relevant planning authority. However, the said regulations and the aforementioned regulations and options shall be applicable from 03.12.2020 onwards
