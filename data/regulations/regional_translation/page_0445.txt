Sir,  
As per your reference letter, I am directed to inform you as follows:  
1) Regulation 11.2.5 of UDCPR 2020 provides for the grant of Amenity Construction TDR. As clarified in the previous letter no.टिपीएस-1221/403/21/नवि-12 dated 17.03.2021, if the work is mandatory/compulsory, then the Construction TDR may be granted. Apart from that,  
            
 i) MRTP Section 2 (2) provides that "Drainage" is included in Amenity. Therefore, this regulation shall apply to it.  
 ii) The owner of the land / the person holding development rights / the tenant shall be liable to pay TDR for the land and construction.  
 iii) If the scope of the ongoing work expands to include a drain or if new work is undertaken, then the TDR may be granted.  
 iv) Clarification regarding commencement is provided in Regulation 2.7.1. In the present case, the work of drainage shall be considered separately even if it is part of the previous road, and it shall be eligible for the existing regulation with a multiplier of 2 as per the applicable regulation.
