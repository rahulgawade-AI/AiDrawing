There is inconsistency in the provisions. Therefore, it is necessary to remove such inconsistency, and in accordance with Regulation No. 3.1.3, open spaces (Open Space) permitted from river bank to green belt, as well as Recreational open space mentioned in Regulation No. 4.12.1(ii), may be proposed for development permission without considering the above-mentioned areas, and such proposed open spaces may be proposed adjacent to river/streams. However, such proposed open spaces must be of appropriate size as per the provisions of the regulations, and such proposed Open Space, Recreational open spaces must maintain a distance of 15.00 meters from river and 9.00 meters from stream, as well as the provisions of Regulation No. 3.1.3 regarding green belt, and the proposed open spaces shall be development permitted as per the provisions of UDCPR.
