Clarification by the Government

1
In accordance with the provision of Regulation No. 3.5.1 of the said regulation, the area of the land under the previously proposed plan is more than 0.40 hectare, therefore, the provision of the facility area is proposed and the approval is granted for the improvement of the plan for the purpose of demarcation. Now, in accordance with the improved provision, the Amenity space is cancelled and the improved plan is filed, therefore, the improved plan is submitted for demarcation.
Maharashtra Regional Planning and Urban Development Act, 1966, provides the provisions for the planning of the development and use of land, and the said Act came into force from 11 January, 1967. Therefore, after 11 January, 1967, if any land has been divided into unauthorized plots without the permission of the competent authority, and even if the area of such plots is less than 2.0 hectare, the area of such unauthorized plots is not considered, and the total area of such land is considered in accordance with Clause 7/12.
