Shri Chief Minister,  
Subject to the said matter, due to discrepancy arising in the implementation of basic area index and maximum development capacity in the area under approved UDCPR, the Chairman, CIDCO, B.E.N.M. Raigad has submitted a petition to the Government on 09.01.2023.  
In the said proposal, the following clarification/guidelines are provided under Regulation No. 10.10.1 of the approved UDCPR, Note No. 3:  
I. Under Regulation No. 10.10.1 of the approved Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations, Note No. 3, SIDCO is allowed to grant additional 0.5 area index along with the basic area index of 1.5 as per the plot allocated for the project and lease, in accordance with Table No. 6 G, as per UDCPR, maximum development capacity (Maximum Building Potential) (i.e. 1.10 basic area index + premium area index + TDR) on the said plot.  
II. In accordance with Regulation No. 10.10.1 (b), for plots larger than 1000 sq. m. and roads wider than 10 m. facing the plot, SIDCO is allowed to grant additional 0.5 area index on the said plot allocated for the project and lease, as per the basic area index of 1.5 under UDCPR.  
It is my order to inform you accordingly.
