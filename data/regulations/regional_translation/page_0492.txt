It will be applicable. Likewise, based on this no-objection certificate, the relevant planning authority will be able to approve 100% transferable development rights / plot area index (FSI).  
Alternative No. 02. The land (landlord class - 2 under the land ownership right) which is reserved in the approved development plan will be required to be amended by the planning authority in the public interest. If at such time the landowner refuses to pay 10% amount to the revenue department, then in such a situation, the amendment process will be completed and the development plan implementation will be carried out. In such a case, the planning authority will deposit 10% amount (as security) with the district collector office with the consent of the landowner, then the landowner will be entitled to retain 10% TDR/FSI and the remaining 10% transferable TDR/FSI will be approved.  
Alternative No. 03. After carrying out the action as per Alternative No. 02, the relevant planning authority will retain the remaining 10% TDR/FSI with the planning authority after approving 10% development right transfer (TDR)/plot area index (FSI) to the landowner. If the landowner requests the planning authority for TDR/FSI, then in such a case, the landowner will be charged interest-bearing amount of the fee from the date of deposit of the security amount to the date of the request. Once the landowner deposits the total amount of the fee to the planning authority, the remaining 10% transferable development right (TDR)/plot area index (FSI) will be transferred to the landowner.  
This directive will come into effect immediately.
