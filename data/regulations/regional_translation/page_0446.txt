2) Regulation 2.7.1 of UDCPR defines "Commencement". The commencement of construction of a building is defined as up to Plinth Level. Even though there is no provision regarding the issuance of Plinth Certificate in UDCPR, the date on which the developer informs the Municipal Corporation about Plinth Completion shall be considered as reference. Considering Regulation 2.7.1 and 11.2.5 together, the following points become clear:-
i) The provisions of the existing regulation applicable on the date of commencement of construction for the purpose of Construction TDR shall apply, that is, if the construction is commenced after the commencement of UDCPR and the Plinth is completed and further construction is carried out, then the provisions of UDCPR shall apply.
ii) In such cases, the TDR shall be allowed at twice the rate for all works subsequent to the Plinth. However, for works carried out prior to the Plinth, the old rate shall apply.
iii) The provision in UDCPR - Table 11-A, ix, that the permissions granted earlier for development under the Accommodation Reservation (A.R.) principle shall continue to be valid in the same manner as per the present approval, is for the purpose of making it perpetual / binding. However, Regulation 11.2.5 shall continue to apply for the determination of how much and how Construction TDR shall be granted. That is, Regulation 11.1 relates to the details of how to develop the reserved land (A.R.), whereas Regulation 11.2.5 shall be considered for the purpose of granting TDR in lieu thereof.
3) The provision in Table 11-A of UDCPR, ix, is as follows - In cases where permission for development under the accommodation reservation principle has already been granted as per earlier regulations, the same shall continue to be valid till completion of construction.
Whereas the scheme of accommodation reservation was approved at that time, it shall continue to be valid in accordance with the existing regulations. This provision shall also apply for determining the extent of land (percentage) and the area to be transferred by way of development on such land, that is, the request for granting 40% land instead of 50% as per new UDCPR shall not be accepted.
