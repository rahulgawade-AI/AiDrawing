6.3 PERMISSIBLE FSI Table 6G Note xii In case plots having approach by dead end road, (point access) the potential of plot mentioned in above table shall be permissible if length of such access road does not exceed 100 m. 
For plots located within 100 meters from the main road having Dead end or point access, the plot area index as per Regulation 6.3, Table 6-G, Note xii is permissible as per main road. Under UDCPR, there is no specific provision regarding the height of buildings in relation to road frontage. It is necessary to consider the provisions of Regulation No.1.3(93)(xiv) in Regulation 3.3.9 regarding access provision for special buildings, the provision regarding building height in Regulation 6.10.1, and such provisions collectively to grant permission. Then, in the case of Pune Municipal Corporation, it is necessary to consider the provision of Regulation 3.3.9 regarding building height, the provision of Regulation 6.10.1 regarding building height, and the specific provision of Regulation 10.1.1 for Pune Municipal Corporation area regarding building height, and such provisions collectively to grant permission.
