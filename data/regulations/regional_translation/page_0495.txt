Regulations have been framed for the purpose of planning, and the said Act came into force on 11 January, 1967. Therefore, from 11 January, 1967 onwards, if any land has been illegally divided without obtaining the approval of the competent authority, and even if the area of such illegal division is less than 2.0 ha., the area of such illegal division shall not be considered, and the total area of such land shall be considered as per Section 7/12, and the amenity area shall be provided as per the existing regulations.  
As per the notification approved, as per the amendment approved on 16.06.2021, in the plan/demarcation of land area of 20,000 sq. m. or more, the provision of 5% amenity area is mandatory. The plans/demarcations approved before 16.06.2021 (provisional or final plan) shall have the amenity area provided as per the existing regulations. As per the regulation 3.5.1 of the said regulations, the amenity area shall not be reduced while amending the plan as per the regulation 3.5.1(viii).
