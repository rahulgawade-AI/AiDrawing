Sir,
Regarding the subject matter, you are requested to refer to your letter No. 1 and 2, wherein the Regulation No. 10.4.3 of Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations applicable to Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority is applicable only for EP.249. Alternatively, the said Regulation may be used in entirety, and guidance is sought from Chief Urban Planner, Maharashtra State, Pune.
As no provision in UDCPR refers to the guidance of the office of the Chief Urban Planner, the opinion of the said office is sought to clarify and provide guidance regarding Regulation No. 1.9 (v) of UDCPR. In this regard, I am directed to inform you as follows.
The Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations, approved by the Government on 02/12/2020, includes a specific regulation (City Specific Regulations) for the city in Part No. 10. The Regulation No. 10.4.3 of Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority, included in the said specific regulation, is clearly stated to be applicable for the area referred to in the notification No. 25.6 (xxxx) issued by the Government regarding the approval of development plan for Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority. Therefore, the said Regulation is applicable only for the area specified in the notification No. EP.249 of the approved development plan for Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority.
