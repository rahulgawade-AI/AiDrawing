Order
No. TPS-1818/Pr. No. 236/18(Part-2)/Navi-13
Date: 23/12/2021
Subject : Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR)
In accordance with Regulation No. 1.9(v) of the approved Regulations, regarding providing clarification.
Whereby, the Government has issued Notification No. TPS 1818/Pr. No. 236/18/Clause 37(1kk)(g) and Clause 20(4)/Navi-13, dated 02/12/2020, approving the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations for all Planning Authorities (except some exceptions) and Regional Plan Areas, which came into force from 03/12/2020;
and whereby, in accordance with Regulation No. 1.9 of the said Regulations, there is a provision regarding the Government issuing orders in relation to the matters/questions raised in connection with the provisions of various provisions of UDCPR;
and whereby, the Government has issued guidance to the concerned parties in accordance with the aforementioned provisions and the timely issued orders;
and whereby, certain difficulties arising in the implementation of other provisions of the said Regulations have been received from the Planning Authorities as well as other organizations;
and whereby, considering the aforementioned various representations and in the interest of public, the Government has decided that it is necessary to review certain provisions of the said Regulations and provide clarification in accordance with Regulation No. 1.9(v) of the said Regulations;
now, therefore, in accordance with the aforementioned provisions of the said Regulations, the detailed explanatory notices are being issued in the attached annexure as per the following actions.
