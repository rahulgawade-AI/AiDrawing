Subject :- Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations
Clarification regarding the authority to rectify errors in architectural drawings...
Reference :- Your letter No. P. No. 44/21, dated 15.07.2021.
Sir,
Regarding the rectification of architectural errors in the approved development plans of some lands in the jurisdiction of the Pernem Municipal Council in Raigad District and the Kulkarni-Badlapur Municipal Council in Thane District, the concerned Municipal Councils have submitted the proposals to the Executive Office. The said proposals include the request for guidance in accordance with the referenced letter.
In this regard, I have the following instructions to inform you.
The Government has approved the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (Unified DCPR) for all planning authorities in the state except for Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation, Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation area, other planning authorities and some specific areas, as well as for regional plans (rural areas) in accordance with the provisions of the Maharashtra Regional Planning and Town Planning Act, 1966, through an official notification dated 02/12/2020, and has made it applicable from 03/12/2020. According to the approved Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (Regulation No. 2.5), the authority to rectify errors in architectural drawings has been granted to the Divisional Commissioner, Town Planning.
In some approved development plan notifications, a note has been included regarding the rectification of architectural errors by the Chief Executive Officer, Town Planning, with the prior approval of the concerned Chief Officer. According to Regulation No. 1.2 of the approved UDCPR, all existing regulations / special regulations are hereby declared to be repealed. However, the provision regarding the rectification of architectural errors is not part of the existing approved regulations but is part of the development plan approval notification. Therefore, such a provision is under Regulation No. 1.2
