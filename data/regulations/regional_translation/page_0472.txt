For the areas, in connection with the provision of basic universal facilities, as per reference no. 1 of the notification, it is the opinion of the government to collect the market value of land. Therefore, the directive no. TPS-2416/Pr. Kr. 43/16/Navi-9, dated 20/4/2016, issued by the Department of Urban Development, has been issued to include a new clause 'k-1' under clause 'k' as follows:
"k-1) The authorities shall, in accordance with the provisions of regulation 2.3 (vi) of the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations, while amending the land use zone, carry out the action of collecting the market value of land as per 'a' above."
The above amended provision is being issued to come into force immediately from the date of this notification to all planning authorities in the state / all branches of the Department of Urban Development / all district collectors / other authorities.
