Correction Order (Order No. 11 in reference)  
No. : TPS-1821/2023/Pr. No. 104/2022/Nav 30, dated 02.06.2023  
Subject : Explanation regarding Regulation No. 3.4.1 and Regulation No. 3.5.1 of the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) ...  
Reference : Government Order No. TPS-1821/2023/Pr. No. 104/2022/Nav-30, dated 29.11.2022 (Explanation Order No. 11)  
Government has issued Notification No. TPS-1818/Pr. No. 236/18/Viyos and Prayos/Kalam 37(1kk)(g) and Kalam 20/Nav-13, dated 02.12.2020, approving the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (hereinafter referred to as "the said Regulations") for all planning authorities (except some exceptions) and regional planning areas, which came into force from 03.12.2020;  
According to Regulation No. 1.9(v) of the said Regulations, there is a provision regarding the issuance of orders by the Government in relation to the interpretation of various provisions of the said Regulations in view of the existing questions/issues. In accordance with this provision, the Government has issued guidance to the concerned parties regarding the interpretation of provisions of the said Regulations in view of the difficulties arising in the implementation of the provisions of Regulation No. 3.4.1(i) and 3.5.1 of the said Regulations;  
In view of the above, it is observed that the explanation provided in the said Government Order No. 11, in the form of Sub-Item No. 1 and 2, does not fully clarify the existing issues and the intended meaning is not clearly conveyed.  
Considering the above, it is necessary to issue a consolidated revised explanation regarding the two issues in place of the explanation provided in Sub-Item No. 1 and 2 of the Government Order dated 29.11.2022, and it is necessary to issue a correction order for this purpose.
