and wherein, in accordance with the provisions of Regulation No. 1.9 (v) of the said Rules, there is a provision regarding the issuance of orders by the Government in relation to the questions/issues in the context of the application of various provisions of UDCPR:

and wherein, in accordance with the provisions of Regulation No. 10.10.1 Note No. 3 of the said Rules, the notifications received from SIDCO and other authorities regarding the difficulties arising in the implementation of such provisions are as follows;
and wherein, considering the aforementioned various notifications and in the interest of public, the Government has formed the opinion that it is necessary to review certain provisions of the said Rules by examining their applicability in order to clarify such provisions in accordance with Regulation No. 1.9 (v) of the said Rules.
Now, therefore, the following explanatory instructions are issued in accordance with Regulation No. 10.10.1 Note No. 3 of the said Rules:
1) Regulation No. 10.10.1 Note No. 3 — The land which was allocated by SIDCO to the project beneficiaries with a Floor Area Ratio (FAR) of 1.5 prior to the implementation of UDCPR shall be allowed a maximum development potential (Maximum Building Potential) (i.e., 1.10 basic FAR + premium FAR + TDR) along with an additional FAR of 0.5. Similarly, the land which was allocated by SIDCO through leasehold with a FAR of 1.5 prior to the implementation of UDCPR shall also be subject to the provisions of Regulation No. 10.10.1 Note No. 3 as mentioned.
2) From the date of coming into force of the UDCPR Rules, SIDCO shall ensure that the Floor Area Ratio or the maximum development potential of the land allocated through leasehold or auction is maintained as per the provisions of UDCPR.
