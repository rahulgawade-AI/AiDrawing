Preface:- The Government has approved the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations through the notification dated 02.12.2020, which came into effect from 03.12.2020. The said regulations apply to all authorities and regional plan areas in the state, except the area mentioned in the notification. Regulation 2.2.14(ii) of the said regulations relates to Fire Infrastructure Charges, and contains the provision that "These charges shall be decided by the Government from time to time".
Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act, 2006 has been implemented from 06.12.2008 for the state of Maharashtra. The said Act contains provisions regarding the collection of charges for fire services, including their increase or decrease, and specifies in detail how such charges should be collected and allocated in a separate fund.
Since the said Act applies to the entire state, the provisions thereof and the charges to be collected as per the said Act are repeated in the approved Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations regarding Fire Infrastructure Charges. In view of the Government's directive, the following orders are hereby issued regarding the collection of Fire Infrastructure Charges as per the provision of Regulation 2.2.14(ii) of the approved Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations.
Order
The collection of Fire Infrastructure Charges as per the provision of Regulation 2.2.14(ii) of the approved Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations shall not be done separately. However, the necessary fees/charges as per the provisions of the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act, 2006 shall be collected and the allocation thereof shall be done as per the provisions of the said Act.
