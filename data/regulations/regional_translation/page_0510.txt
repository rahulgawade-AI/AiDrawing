In the above subject matter case, you are requested to keep the said Regulation No.3.4.5 and Regulation No.4.12 of UDCPR as per the reference letter, and to express the opinion that the open space can be proposed within Green Belt/River Protection Belt. You are advised to inform the following:
"Under Regulation No.3.1.3(i)(a) of the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR), the area from river bank to green belt is prohibited zone. However, the use of open space, garden, lawns etc. in the said area is allowed. If Green Belt or River Protection Belt is proposed as use zone adjacent to river/kanal bank, then under Regulation No.4.12.1(ii) of UDCPR, the use of open space is allowed without considering the distance of 15 m. and 9.0 m. from river/kanal. Also, under Regulation No.4.12(v) and 4.12(vii) of UDCPR, there are provisions to leave 15 m. from river and 9.00 m. from kanal while proposing Recreational Open Space in the said use zone.
