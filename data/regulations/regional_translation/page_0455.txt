Sir,
In view of the above subject, there is ambiguity in the provisions of Regulation 6.3 and Regulation 11.2.7(iii), therefore, we are requesting for guidance from the Government.
In this regard, I am informing you as follows:
01. Regulation 6.3, Table 6-G Note (xiv) and Regulation 11.2.7 are different provisions and are self-contained.
The provisions of Regulation 6.3 and Table 6-G thereof relate to the maximum allowable building capacity on a plot. According to Note (xiv), the basic FSI (floor space index) shall be allowed on the net plot area remaining after excluding the area under development plan road/route, utility area/ reservation. However, if the area under development plan road/route or utility area/reservation is transferred to the authority, then the total plot area shall be considered and the capacity for Premium FSI & TDR loading shall be allowed accordingly.
02. Regulation 11.2.7 relates to the direct use of TDR. According to sub-clause (i), the maximum allowable building capacity on a plot and the use of TDR shall be allowed as per the provisions of Regulation 6.1, 6.2, 6.3. However, according to sub-clause (iii), the direct use of TDR shall be allowed on the net plot area remaining after excluding the areas affected by development plan road/route, reservation/utility area (utility space).
