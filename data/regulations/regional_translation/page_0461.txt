10.4 Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority
10.4.1 Development along Ring Road:
          250 m. Residential Zone/ Residential Belt proposed along the 60 m. wide Outer Ring Road as a corridor development is sanctioned, subject to payment of premium. The development in this 250 m. corridor is permitted on payment of premium as decided by the Government on the total area of land under development or building permission. Such premium shall be deposited with the concerned Authority.

In the said provision, the Government shall fix the rate of premium as decided by the Government accordingly, and the provision of development permission is made. The Government has previously issued order No. TPS-2416/Pr. No. 122(Part-3)/2016/New-9, dated 20/11/2018, approving the Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Plan and Development Control and Promotion Regulations, wherein the rate of premium for development proposals proposed on both sides of the 60 m. wide Outer Ring Road as 250 m. Residential Zone/Residential Belt has been fixed at 15%. The same rate shall be applicable for the said development proposals in accordance with the said regulations.

2.2 Procedure for Obtaining Development Permission / Building Permission / Commencement Certificate
2.2.12 Building / Layout Permission Scrutiny Fee -
          The notice shall be accompanied by a self-attested copy of receipt of payment of building/ layout permission Scrutiny Fee. These fees shall be as mentioned below and shall be subject to Government orders, if any. Provided that, such fees shall not be applicable for the development proposals implemented by Government / Government Departments or Public Authorities of State or Central Government.

In accordance with the provision of Section 18(1)(ii) of the Maharashtra Regional and Town Planning Act, 1966, the Gram Panchayat shall grant development permission in the Regional Plan Area and Village Panchayat Area. Such development permission shall be granted by the Gram Panchayat, after obtaining prior permission from the Town Planning Officer at the level of Panchayat Samiti or District Panchayat. However, in the current situation, since the Town Planning Officer's appointment at the level of Panchayat Samiti or District Panchayat has not been made, the Gram Panchayat shall obtain prior approval from the branch office of the Town Planning Department of the respective district before granting development permission. Therefore, the scrutiny of such related development permission cases shall be carried out at the level of the branch office of the respective district. Hence, in accordance with Regulation 2.2.12, the Building / Layout Permission Scrutiny Fee shall be deposited with the branch office of the Town Planning Department of the respective district instead of the Gram Panchayat.

2.2.13 Development Charges
          Development charges as required under Section 124 A of the Maharashtra Regional and Town Planning Act, 1966 shall be deposited with the Authority before issue of development permission/ commencement certificate. Such charges shall be calculated for area of each land parcel included in the development permission considering the rates in ASR and provisions mentioned in the said Act.
          Provided that,
          i) to ix) -----

In case the development permission has been revoked after paying the development charges, there is a provision to adjust the said charges during the revised permission. The development charges paid during the development permission are based on the use and area of the respective land/ construction, and are not related to the ownership rights. Therefore, any development charges collected in any development permission are related to the respective land. Hence, even if the said land is sold by the original owner, the new owner shall also be able to adjust the said charges.
