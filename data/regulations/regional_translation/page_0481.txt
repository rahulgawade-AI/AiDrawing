While referring to the SIDCO area, only the area of SIDCO at Navi Mumbai should be considered, similarly, while referring to the MADC area, only the area of Shirdi Airport should be considered, or if there are areas where MADC has notified areas, those should be considered. The Controller, Municipal Planning, Maharashtra State, Pune has requested the Government for clarification. 
And in this regard, there is a provision in the said regulations, Regulation No. 1.9, sub-clause (v) as per the said provisions of UDCPR, regarding the issuance of orders by the Government in relation to the existing questions/issues; and in this regard, the Government has issued guidance to the concerned persons in accordance with the said provisions; and in this regard, the Controller's request has been considered and in the interest of public, the Government has decided that it is necessary for the Government to issue clarification regarding the said regulations in accordance with Regulation No. 1.9 (v) of the said regulations;
Now, in this regard, in accordance with Section 37(1ka) of the said Act, by virtue of the published advisory, the following clarificatory advisory is being issued:
1) In the said advisory dated 2/12/2020, wherever the reference is made to "SIDCO Area (for which SIDCO Planning Authority is responsible)", it is expected that the said regulations should be applicable to the area where SIDCO Planning Authority is responsible. In the State, SIDCO is, in some areas, the Planning Authority under the "New Town Development Authority (NTDA)" and in some areas, it is the Planning Authority under the "Special Planning Authority (SPA)". Therefore, the said regulations shall remain applicable to all areas where SIDCO is the Planning Authority under the "New Town Development Authority (NTDA)" or the "Special Planning Authority (SPA)".
2) In the State, wherever the said Act's Section 40 has notified the area and appointed MADC as the Special Planning Authority (SPA) for that area, the said regulations notified under Section 154 of the said Act shall remain applicable to all such areas.
