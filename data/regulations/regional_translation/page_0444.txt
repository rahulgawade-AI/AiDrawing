UDCPR-2020
Cases regarding concession in distance from Pump Junction are received by the Divisional Office. The request for guidance and determination of policy has been made by the Additional Chief Development Officer, Pune Division, Pune, after consultation with the Department of Public Works. 
The actual approved UDCPR provisions are clear. According to that, while granting permission for a petrol pump on a classified road, the distance from the junctions of the roads should be considered as per the specifications mentioned by IRC/MoRTH. Therefore, it is necessary to consider the provisions of the approved regulations while granting permission for a petrol pump. The distance from the junction is a subject of the Department of Public Works' jurisdiction, and the authority of the Department of Public Works to relax the distance as per the specifications mentioned by IRC/MoRTH in the approved regulations is present. Hence, it is expected to grant permission considering the distance as per the relaxation provided by that department.
Therefore, it is directed to carry out the subsequent action at our level considering the above-mentioned situation.
