Subject :- Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR)
Regarding clarification regarding Regulation No. 14.2.1 of the approved Regulations.
Sir,
Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations have been approved with Regulation No. 14.2.1 - Transit Oriented Development for Pune Municipal Corporation area being kept in abeyance as mentioned, the said Regulations have been approved. It has come to the notice of the Government that there is a question regarding the implementation of the said Regulation which is kept in abeyance in the approved UDCPR.
In view of the above-mentioned question, I have been directed to provide the clarification as follows under Regulation No. 1.9(v):
"Regulation No. 14.2.1 of the approved Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) is kept in abeyance. However, the Government has issued the amended Regulation through Notification No. TPS-1818/150/PR. No. 158/19/Navi-13, dated 30/06/2020, under Section 37(1ka) of the Maharashtra Regional Planning and Town Planning Act, 1966, for amendment of Regulation No. 24.8 of the previously approved Development Control and Promotion Regulations-2017. The amended Regulation has been published and implemented immediately on 30/06/2020, as per the directions under Section 154. Therefore, the Regulation implemented as per the said directions shall remain in force. That is, the approved TOD Regulation shall continue to be in force as before."
