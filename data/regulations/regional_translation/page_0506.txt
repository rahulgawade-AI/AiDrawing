• Regulation No. 14.2.1.2.4 (ii) —
In accordance with Regulation No. 14.2.1.2.4 (ii) of UDCPR, after deducting the transferable development rights (TDR) from the floor space index (FSI), the remaining additional FSI shall be allocated as follows: 25% of the FSI shall be allocated through TDR and 75% of the FSI shall be allocated through premium.
However, if the developer/landowner has requested to allocate the in-situ FSI/DR (Development Rights) in lieu of dividing the remaining FSI into 25% TDR and 75% FSI, then the in-situ FSI/DR shall be adjusted in the 25% TDR component and the remaining 75% FSI shall be allocated through premium, provided that the quality of the case is considered and the in-situ FSI is allocated without the intervention of DRC (Development Rights Committee).
