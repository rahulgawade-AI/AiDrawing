**Section 2**  
*9.13 PROVISION OF PODIUM*  
*9.13.1 Planning and Design: At least one lift -----, it will not be necessary to raise the existing lift to the additional floor.*  

**Regulation 9.13** *As per the said regulation, 1 fire lift shall be allowed for buildings with height between 15.0 m and 24.0 m.*  

---

**Section 3**  
*9.27 PROVISION OF LIFT*  
*9.27.1 Planning and Design: At least one lift -----, it will not be necessary to raise the existing lift to the additional floor.*  

**Regulation 9.27** *For buildings with height exceeding 15.0 m and up to 24.0 m, 1 fire lift shall be allowed.*  

---

**Section 4**  
*9.28 STAIRCASE REQUIREMENTS*  
*9.28.1 Planning and Design: At least one lift -----, it will not be necessary to raise the existing lift to the additional floor.*  

**Regulation 9.28** *For buildings with height exceeding 15.0 m and up to 24.0 m, 1 fire lift shall be allowed. For special buildings exceeding 24.0 m, a separate fire staircase shall be proposed as per UDCPR.*

**Section 5**  
*UDCPR पूर्वी मंजूर झालेल्या तसेच भागशः / पूर्णतः भोगवटा प्रमाणपत्र मिळालेल्या अथवा अद्यापि न मिळालेल्या इमारतीसाठी तत्कालिन नियमानुसार १.२० मी. रुंद जिना (Staircase) अस्तित्वात असेल आणि आता UDCPR नुसार मिळणाऱ्या चटई क्षेत्र निर्देशांकानुसार अतिरिक्त बांधकाम अनुज्ञेय होऊ शकते, परंतु इमारतीची उंची वाढून त्या उंचीसाठी सदर विनियमातील तरतुदीनुसार १.५० मी. रुंदीचा जिना आवश्यक असेल.*  

**Regulation 9.28** *For buildings with height exceeding 15.0 m and up to 24.0 m, 1 fire lift shall be allowed. For special buildings exceeding 24.0 m, a separate fire staircase shall be proposed as per UDCPR. However, if the existing staircase width of 1.20 m is maintained in accordance with current regulations, and the building height increases to meet the guidelines under UDCPR, the required staircase width of 1.50 m shall be mandatory. If structural modifications (e.g., beams, columns) are not feasible, the staircase width of 1.20 m shall be allowed for any floor, provided the total floor area is less than 750 sq.m. as per Regulation 9.28.8, Table 9-G, Sub-item 1(d).*
